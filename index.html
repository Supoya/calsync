calsync

calsync is a personal, self-hosted tool that keeps the owner's own iCloud calendar and Google Calendar in sync.

Privacy policy

calsync is used only by its owner. It reads and writes calendar events solely between the owner's own calendars. No data is collected, stored on third-party servers, shared, or sold. Credentials stay on the owner's own device.

这是个人自用的日历同步工具，只在本人的日历之间同步，不收集、不分享任何数据。
